Overview

  • The POS Purchase Order module bridges the gap between Point of Sale and Purchasing in Odoo 18.

    This module adds a Purchase Order button to the POS control buttons, allowing users to quickly create purchase orders directly from the POS interface. When clicked, it opens the purchase order form in a new browser tab, enabling seamless procurement without leaving the POS workflow.

    This is particularly useful for retail businesses that need to reorder stock on-the-fly while serving customers, or for scenarios where POS operators need to initiate vendor orders based on real-time inventory observations.

    Perfect for businesses that want quick access to procurement from their Point of Sale stations.

    Developed and maintained by OdooElevate.

Key Features

  • Quick Purchase Order Button

    Adds a dedicated Purchase Order button directly in the POS control buttons area for instant access to procurement.

  • One-Click Access

    Single click opens the purchase order form in a new tab. No interruption to your current POS session or customer service.

  • On-the-Fly Reordering

    Perfect for retail businesses that need to reorder stock while serving customers at the point of sale.

  • Native Integration

    Fully integrates with Odoo's POS control buttons and Purchase module. No additional configuration required.

  • Seamless Workflow

    Creates purchase orders without leaving the POS interface. Your POS session continues uninterrupted in the original tab.

  • Multi-Language

    Available in 6 languages: English, French, Spanish, German, Chinese, and Arabic with RTL support.

How It Works

A simple two-step process to create purchase orders from POS

Step 1: Click Actions

In the POS interface, click the Actions button at the bottom of the screen

Step 2: Click Purchase Order

Select the Purchase Order button from the Actions popup to open a new PO form in a new tab
